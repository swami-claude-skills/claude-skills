This page is the skill sheet for the Fusion palette add-in skill. It builds an Autodesk Fusion add-in to Swami's house spec: a dockable dark panel with a version in its title, saved settings with a [reset](/ɹˈiːsɛt/), and installers for the Mac and for Windows

Section one, Dashboard Summary. A short intro and a table of every section.

Section two, Search. Type a few words to find anything on this page, in any order and with small typos forgiven. Narrow the results with filters, and click a result to jump to it.

Section three, What It Is. What the skill is, and why one fixed house spec means no tool ships without its version, its [reset](/ɹˈiːsɛt/), its error strip, or its installer.

Section four, What It Does. Nine jobs, from asking for the name, location, and idea, through the code, the palette, the manifest, and the icon, to settings, installers, a [preflight](/pɹˈiːflaɪt/) check, and the zip.

Section five, How to Use It. Eight steps on the Mac, from asking for the tool to setting its keyboard shortcut, then six steps on Windows, where you [extract](/ɪkstɹˈækt/) the zip first.

Section six, Skills It Depends On. Two required skills: Fusion add-in scaffold for the Python rules, and Fusion add-in installer for the installers. Vibe dashboard is related, for write-ups.

Section seven, Programs It Depends On. Autodesk Fusion on your computer, plus Python, Pillow, zip, and bash in Claude's workspace. Terminal runs the Mac installer and PowerShell the Windows one.

Section eight, Capabilities and Limitations. It delivers a whole checked package that stays open while you work and keeps its settings. But Claude can't test it inside Fusion, and you set the keyboard shortcut yourself.

Section nine, Scheduled Tasks. None. The nightly skill library refresh only packs it into a zip.

Section ten, Where It Runs. Swami runs it in the Claude desktop app linked to his Mac. The other surfaces should work too, but are untested.

Section eleven, Gotchas. Fourteen traps, from mismatched names and forgotten version bumps to lost handlers, half [resets](/ɹˈiːsɛts/), Gatekeeper, and Windows execution policy.

Section twelve, Other Requirements. Fusion on a Mac or on Windows ten or eleven, code execution in Claude, and the two companion skills. No admin rights are needed.

Section thirteen, Card and Freshness. Which version of the skill this page describes, and its fingerprint.

Section fourteen, Mind Map. The whole page as bubbles you can open, hover for a summary, and click to jump to that spot.

Section fifteen, Glossary. The terms used on this page, each linked to where it appears.

Section sixteen, Source Document, Verbatim. The skill's own instructions, word for word.
