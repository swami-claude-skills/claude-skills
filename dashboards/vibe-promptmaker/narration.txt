This page is the skill sheet for the vibe prompt maker skill. It builds an AI prompt maker: a one page form where you fill in fields and get clean plain text, ready to paste into an AI.

Section one, Dashboard Summary. A short intro and a table of every section.

Section two, Search. Type a few words to find anything on this page, in any order and with small typos forgiven. Narrow the results with filters, and click a result to jump to it.

Section three, What It Is. What the skill is and why it exists: a fill in form keeps every case in the same shape, and plain text pastes cleanly into any AI.

Section four, What It Does. Nine features, from the input form and the lyve output, through editing, reordering, and switching fields between one value and a list, to copy, download, and [reset](/ɹˈiːsɛt/) with undo.

Section five, How to Use It. Six steps, from asking for a prompt maker to copying the text into your AI chat. On Windows the steps are the same, with notes on where downloads land.

Section six, Skills It Depends On. The artifact capabilities skill for the download permission, plus the vibe dashboard skill, which shares its design.

Section seven, Programs It Depends On. Just a web browser, and a text editor if you download the text. No Python is needed.

Section eight, Capabilities and Limitations. It works for any kind of case and turns it into clean text. But it keeps nothing between visits, has no [import](/ˈɪmpɔːɹt/), and outputs text only.

Section nine, Scheduled Tasks. None. Its pages are simply backed up each night with all the others.

Section ten, Where It Runs. It works in any Claude app session. Windows, mobile, Claude Code, and other assistants are untested.

Section eleven, Gotchas. Six traps, from losing your typing on reload to blocked pop ups and blocked downloads.

Section twelve, Other Requirements. Artifacts with the download permission, and any current browser on the Mac or on Windows.

Section thirteen, Card and Freshness. Which version of the skill this page describes, and its fingerprint.

Section fourteen, Mind Map. The whole page as bubbles you can open, hover for a summary, and click to jump to that spot.

Section fifteen, Glossary. The terms used on this page, each linked to where it appears.

Section sixteen, Source Document, Verbatim. The skill's own instructions, word for word.
